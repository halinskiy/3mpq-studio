Privacy Policy

Quirky · Effective 25 May 2026

Summary

Quirky does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything the app captures (screen regions, recognized text, picked colors, pixel measurements) is processed locally on your Mac and stays on your Mac. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no network calls.

What Quirky accesses on your Mac

  • Screen Recording — granted via macOS System Settings. Required to draw the capture overlay and to read pixels from the screen region you select. Pixel data is held in memory only for the duration of the capture and is immediately discarded after the result (recognized text, color hex, or measurement) is produced.
  • Clipboard (Pasteboard) — Quirky writes the result of a capture (text, hex string, or measurement label) to the system clipboard so you can paste it. Quirky never reads the existing clipboard contents.

What Quirky does NOT do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (Mac App Store version).
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party services, no cloud sync, no servers operated by us.
  • No network requests of any kind in the Mac App Store build (the com.apple.security.app-sandbox entitlement is enforced and the bundle does not ship an updater framework).
  • No background activity when the app is not actively capturing.

Children

Quirky is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy — for example, to add an optional cloud sync feature — we will update the effective date above and describe the change. Material changes will also be highlighted in the app's release notes.
